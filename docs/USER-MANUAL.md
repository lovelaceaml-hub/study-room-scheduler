# User Manual

## Reserve a room or other resource

Open https://lvrooms.web.app, choose a day and resource, then tap an open time
or choose **Reserve**. Follow the steps and confirm the details. Your browser
remembers your name on that device. **My reservations** shows bookings made
from that device, where you can cancel or end an eligible reservation early.

## Priority and Force override

Contact and New Member reservations have priority. When every room, whiteboard
or TV is taken at the time you need, a Contact reservation can take one held by
a New Member or Other reservation, and a New Member reservation can take one
held by Other. The app shows who is affected and asks you to confirm first.

**Force override** is for a reservation you know is no longer needed. Choose
**Force override** in the time list, or open that reservation on the schedule,
then confirm the warning. It can replace any member's reservation.

In both cases:

- A reservation that already started keeps its place and ends when yours starts.
- Otherwise it moves to another free one, or is replaced if none is free.
- The other person sees what happened, and why, in **My reservations**.
- A reservation marked **Reserved** (held by Admin) is never taken.
- Cancelling your reservation later does not bring the other one back.

## View the lobby schedule

Open https://lvrooms.web.app/tv.html. The page shows current room status,
upcoming reservations, and the day's timeline. It refreshes automatically and
shows short public names, not full names or private notes.

## Admin

Open https://lvrooms.web.app/admin/ and enter the Admin PIN. Admin can review
and search reservations, add or manage bookings, keep private notes, and
remove old records. Lock the page when finished. The page locks automatically
after inactivity.

## Connection and freshness

The member and Admin pages refresh automatically while open. A booking or
Admin change refreshes immediately. Admin stops background polling while its
page is hidden and refreshes when you return if the data is at least 30
seconds old. The lobby TV refreshes every minute and keeps its last schedule
available when the connection is lost.

## Help and privacy

Do not use a branch preview to make changes because it connects to the live
backend. Do not share the Admin PIN or enter it on a preview or unfamiliar
page. For questions about a reservation, contact the church administrator.
