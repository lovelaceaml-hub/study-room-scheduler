# Using the Study Room Scheduler

Open [lvrooms.web.app](https://lvrooms.web.app) to reserve a room, whiteboard,
or TV. The [Lobby TV](https://lvrooms.web.app/tv.html) shows today's schedule.

## Make a reservation

1. Choose a day within the next three days.
2. Choose an available time and resource, then enter the requested contact or
   member details.
3. Review the reservation and confirm. It is active immediately.

Each browser remembers its own reservations. Open **My reservations** on the
same device to cancel or end one early. Ending early releases the unused time.

## See today's schedule

Open the Lobby TV page. It shows the public schedule and short display names,
not full names or private notes.

## Help and privacy

The Admin page is for authorized administrators. It requires the protected
administrator PIN. Do not share the PIN or enter it on a preview or other
unfamiliar page. A preview may connect to the live schedule.

For questions about a reservation, contact the church administrator.
