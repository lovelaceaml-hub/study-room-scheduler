# Cove

Calm sea-glass material creates a welcoming place to find a moment together. A humanist system sans keeps every part of the experience gentle and readable.

## Signature

Four continuous translucent column wells sit inside a curved white schedule tray. Occupied reservations float as soft colored pebbles. Subtle plus and Open buttons retain the exact room and half-hour start the person touches.

On a phone, a segmented resource island and compact date chip lead directly into the schedule. A frosted floating action capsule stays within reach. On desktop, the schedule sits beside a narrow sticky invitation to reserve.

Soft dissolves explain resource and flow changes. A quiet ripple acknowledges selection and the simulated booking. Reduced motion disables the animations.

## Behavior

- Full 10 AM to 11 PM schedule with one left hour rail on slot boundaries.
- Four Study Rooms, five Whiteboards, three TVs, and the upcoming Tuesday and Saturday.
- Saturday services at 10 to 11 AM, 2:30 to 3:30 PM, and 7:30 to 8:30 PM. Tuesday service at 7:30 to 8:30 PM.
- General Reserve Now asks for the resource first. Direct open slots preserve their exact resource, unit, and start and begin with purpose.
- Purpose, start where needed, valid end, fictional name, confirmation, and visible simulated success. Endings require one unit free for the whole range and cannot cross a service.
- A native dialog provides modal keyboard behavior, explicit focus containment, Escape dismissal, focus return, and screen-reader labels.
- Query parameters: `?state=loading`, `?state=empty`, and `?state=error`, each with a visible recovery action. Optional `?day=tue`, `?day=sat`, and `?resource=whiteboard` or `?resource=tv`.

## Boundaries and anti-goals

All reservations and names are fictional sample data. Test bookings live only in browser memory and disappear on refresh. The prototype contains no Firebase, network, or browser-storage calls.

Avoid serif hospitality styling, editorial paper, rose date tickets, hard table borders, dark operation panels, decorative artwork, and resource cards that consume the schedule's space.
