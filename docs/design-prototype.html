Chosen direction

Warm, focused, and honest.

The paper-like palette gives the scheduler a recognizable identity. Availability is the primary information; numbered room occupancy is supporting detail because members request a resource type and Admin assigns the exact unit.

Interaction rules shown here

  • One tap selects 30 minutes.
  • A later tap extends the range within the same service window.
  • Pending demand never appears inside a numbered room.
  • Full periods remain visible but cannot be selected.
  • Saturday says “Send request”; Tuesday says “Reserve now.”
