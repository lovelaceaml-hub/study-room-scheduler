LV Zion Study Rooms

Three looks for the new scheduler

Each direction shows the same three screens with the new features in place: the desktop schedule for this Sabbath, the phone sign-up screen, and the phone schedule with "My requests." Scroll through all three, then tell Claude which one to build — or mix them ("the layout of one, the colors of another").

What's new in all three

  • One Sabbath at a time — members only see the upcoming Saturday, with a quiet "View last Sabbath" reference link.
  • The approver assigns rooms — members just ask for "a study room" (with an optional preferred room), and the approver places them.
  • A visible waiting line — when every room at a time is taken, up to three backups queue in order (1st, 2nd, 3rd), shown to everyone. Backups get promoted if someone cancels.
  • Confirmed or Tentative — every request says how sure the visit is, it shows on the schedule, and it guides the approver's picks.
  • Easy cancel — "Plans changed? Cancel" on your own requests, even after approval.
  • Tap-to-pick times — tap the start slot, tap the end slot, the range fills in. No more dropdowns.
  • Real equipment inventory — Whiteboards 1–3 (large) and 4–5 (vertical), each with its matching numbered podium, plus TVs 1–3. Rooms already have their own equipment built in.
